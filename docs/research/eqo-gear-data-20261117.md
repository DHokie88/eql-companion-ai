# Gear data verification, November 17, 2026

- **Catalog.** The [EQLWiki Eye of Zomm manifest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Maergoth/EQL-EOZ/dataset/manifest.json) returned pack `20261117T033317Z-e66fe7a60559`. Oracle's existing download and decoder verified SHA-256 `87a638370e4c8c2518e5fc0b5e68d81f34e6c1136c304145a1c393f92cb484f1`. The pack has 11,397 usable item pages. Merged with the bundled data, it yields 6,970 Gear rows, up from 6,967 with the November 1 pack. The pack is community data and its generation date is not a verification date for every item.
- **Base damage parsing.** [Burning Fighters Blade, revision 200399](https://eqlwiki.com/index.php?title=Burning_Fighters_Blade&oldid=200399) lists DMG 8 and Fire DMG 4. The older parser replaced DMG 8 with 4. Fourteen distinct pages in the new pack contain the affected elemental or bane damage syntax. Oracle now reads those fields separately, including for previously parsed records with the original stat text.
- **Upgrade estimates.** [EQLWiki scaling revision 4](https://github.com/Maergoth/EQLWiki/tree/9ec24e99c0d28673b2e3ba046436db5ce76a57f1/extensions/ItemLevelSlider) documents the shared wiki and Eye of Zomm model and its screenshot evidence. Thornstinger's base HP 50 at tier 5 plus 4/32 gives 75, versus 76 under Oracle's old rounding. The upstream notes flag an unresolved base AC discrepancy on Lustrous Russet Vambraces and describe Backstab as a working model. Tier estimates remain conditional on accurate base values.
- **Direct observations.** The standard inventory export exposes item names, IDs, locations and full tiers. It supplies neither inspect stats nor partial progress. Current in-game item windows, with exact tier and progress, are the strongest available evidence for disputed values. [EQ Legends Tools](https://eqlegends.tools/items/) says it transcribes stats from the game. Use observations to cross-check individual discrepancies before relying on that catalog as a second source.
- **Open reports.** The November 17 inventory export lists Augmentor’s Mask in the Hoard and Golden Efreeti Greaves on the Equipment keyring, both without a tier suffix. Neither wiki record changed between the November 1 and November 17 packs. The reported differences could reflect the item’s current tier or the catalog’s base stats. The inventory rows and the Greaves name screenshot cannot resolve which field is wrong. Use complete in-game inspect values and the tier/progress as verification before altering either base record.
